# Meridian Group HR AnalYsis

### Overview
This project is an end-to-end HR analytics and workforce reporting dashboard built in Microsoft Excel. It analyzes employee demographics, workforce composition, attrition trends, retention metrics, and training performance using interactive KPI cards and dashboard visualizations.

The analysis is based on a synthetic HR dataset of 5,500+ employee records designed to simulate real-world enterprise workforce analytics scenarios.

The project was developed to reflect executive-level HR reporting and support workforce planning, employee retention analysis, and training effectiveness evaluation. It also includes interactive navigation buttons for seamless movement across dashboard sections.

### Key Insights

#### 1. Workforce Distribution
Marketing recorded the highest active workforce with 422 employees, while Risk & Compliance had the lowest departmental headcount at 379 employees.

#### 2. Workforce Structure
Mid-level employees formed the largest workforce segment with 1,064 employees, while executive-level staff represented the smallest employee group.

#### 3. Employment Composition
Permanent employees accounted for 79.5% of the workforce, indicating a highly stable employment structure across the organization.

#### 4. Attrition Analysis
End-of-contract exits and resignations emerged as the leading causes of employee exits, highlighting both contractual turnover and voluntary attrition patterns.

#### 5. Retention Performance
The organization maintained an overall retention rate of 79.7%, indicating moderate workforce stability.

#### 6. Tenure Trends
A significant proportion of employees exited within their first year, suggesting potential onboarding, engagement, or retention challenges during early employment stages.

#### 7. Training Performance
Performance Management programs achieved the highest performance improvement levels, while Excel & Data Analytics programs recorded comparatively lower improvement outcomes.
